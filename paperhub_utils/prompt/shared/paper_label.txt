# paper_label
Provide a single label in format `{first_author}{second_author}{year}{topic_keyword}` (all lowercase, no spaces)
- Single author: `melitz2003heterogeneous`
- Two authors: `cardkrueger1994minimumwage`
- Three+ authors: `autoretal2020trade`

The topic keyword should be a brief descriptor of the paper's main subject, try to be as specific as possible.
