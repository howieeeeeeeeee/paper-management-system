# paper_label
Provide one Hybrid PascalCase label using ASCII letters, numbers, and the `_etal` marker only; do not use spaces.
- Single author: `{Surname}{Year}{TopicWords}`, for example `Melitz2003HeterogeneousFirms`.
- Two authors: `{Surname1}{Surname2}{Year}{TopicWords}`, for example `AnDu2026MoralAlignment`.
- Three or more authors: `{Surname1}_etal{Year}{TopicWords}`, for example `Huynh_etal2026LLMCooperation`.

Transliterate accents to ASCII. Write surname and topic words in PascalCase, but preserve familiar acronyms from the title or metadata in uppercase, such as `AI`, `LLM`, and `RCT`. Keep the topic brief, specific, and recognizable.
