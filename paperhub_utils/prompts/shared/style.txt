**IMPORTANT STYLE GUIDELINES (apply throughout all sections):**
- Be concise and precise. Avoid filler words, exaggeration, and academic jargon.
- Write in plain, direct language. Say what the paper does, not how groundbreaking it is.
- Keep sentences short. Prefer bullet points over long paragraphs.
- Only mention relevance to the reader's research if there is a strong, specific connection. Do not force connections.
